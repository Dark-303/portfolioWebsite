FRC Team ALT-F4

A continuation of a great experience

For the past 2 years I have been fortunate enough to be a part of FIRST Robotics Competition team 7558 ALT-F4. This experience is made even better with former alumni from Ctrl-Z also being on this team. ALT-F4 is a world class team and has been on the world stage multiple times. In 2026 we were able to win our field for district championships in Ontario, but narrowly lost to the other field's alliance becoming finalists overall.


I am part of the robot programming team and I additionally help out the head scout with the team's scouting app. This team has many great mentors and coaches that helped me learn and develop new skills. For example, I learned complex math concepts that are far more advanced than the ones I learned in FIRST LEGO League such as vectors and different types of acceleration graphs. Being able to work with metal robots that do not have the errors that a toy like LEGO might have, allows me to develop a stronger understanding of how programming can be applied to real robots.
