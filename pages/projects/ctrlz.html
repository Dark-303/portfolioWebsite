An Alum of FLL Team 5831 Ctrl-Z Bayview Glen

A fun way to learn many skills

As a student, throughout my middle school career I was fortunate to be a member of FIRST LEGO Leauge team 5831 Ctrl-Z Bayview Glen. Here I learned and developed various skills such as presentation skills and how to design products and solutions to real world problems.


FIRST LEGO Leauge has been one of the most important experiences in my life, whether it be a through learning hard skills or transferable ones that will stick with me. It has opened many doors of opportunity, including the ability to be better prepared for future grades. It has given me a basis for all my future works and projects, and has allowed me to grow as a person.
