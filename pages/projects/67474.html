Mentor of FLL Team 67474

An experience to grow and learn as a leader

After middle school, I have begun to mentor FIRST LEGO League team 67474. This gives former FLL Students a wonderful way to give back to the community and make an impact on their younger peers. Team 67474 consists entirely of grade 5 students, and the fact that they have gone to the provincial stages in both their first and second years is where the passage of knowledge really shows. This opportunity as a mentor allows me to grow my presentation skills, leadership, and teaching skills while teaching others to do the same.


Team 67474 under the guidance of the senior coaches as well as student mentors, such as me, has flourished into a young successful team. In their rookie year they were able to achieve the second place innovation project award. In their second year they were awarded the first place innovation award. As a mentor I've taught various subjects that were taught to me in previous years. This includes proportional integral derivative (PID) feedback loops, engineering concepts, presentation and public speaking skills, and many others.
